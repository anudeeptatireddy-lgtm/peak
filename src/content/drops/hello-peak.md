peak is a place to see an animation you love, play with it, and copy the prompt that makes your own version with Claude Code.

Start with the particle text in the hero: drag through it, change the sliders, then copy the prompt. The Gallery has every animation, the Tour plays them back to back, and the guides walk you through setting up Claude Code and putting your page online.

A new animation or color world will land here most weeks.
