Smoke

Move your cursor, or drag a finger sideways, to pour soft coloured smoke. Faster strokes make bigger swirls. Leave it alone and a slow plume rises from below.

Paused because your device is set to reduce motion. Press Play to start the smoke.

How the smoke moves

The screen is covered by a coarse, invisible grid. Every frame, each cell works out how the air around it is flowing using Jos Stam’s “stable fluids” method, and a little extra spin keeps the curls lively. Three inks ride along on that flow and slowly fade away.

The colour drifts from pink to blue to orange, five seconds each, and is laid down with a multiply blend, the way printing inks build up on paper.
