Living wallpaper

Press and hold anywhere to stir a whirlpool. Hold a finger still for a moment, then move it to stir.

About this wallpaper

Thousands of ink-colored specks drift along an invisible current. The current is made of three slow, overlapping waves, so it bends and shifts over time, and every speck simply follows the direction the current points where it is.

Nothing is ever erased. Each frame is washed with a thin layer of paper color, so older strokes fade into soft trails, and where pink, blue and orange cross they multiply like ink on paper.

Press and hold anywhere on the wallpaper to stir a whirlpool. On a touch screen, hold your finger still for a moment first; a quick swipe still scrolls the page.
