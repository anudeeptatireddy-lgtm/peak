Black hole

Press and hold to drag the hole around. Click or tap to release a burst of particles.

What you are looking at

Every speck is pulled toward the hole, and the pull gets stronger the closer it gets, so particles whip around faster as they fall in. A little friction makes each orbit shrink until the particle crosses the pink ring and disappears, and a new one drifts in from off-screen.

The faint grid is bent by the same pull, which is why it sinks into a funnel around the hole.
