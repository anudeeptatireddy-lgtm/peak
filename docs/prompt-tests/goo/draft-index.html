Gooey blobs

Blobs drift in slow orbits and melt together when they touch. Move your pointer over them and a pink blob follows; click or tap to drop a new one (up to 16).

About the animation: on a pale lavender background, round blobs in hot pink, bright blue and orange circle slowly around the middle of the screen. When two blobs come close they stretch toward each other and merge into one smooth, gooey shape, then pull apart again as they drift on.
