# Testing peak with friends

A 60 to 90 minute session with someone who has **never used Claude Code**. The goal is to find where a real beginner gets stuck, not to show them how clever the site is. Run it with 3 to 5 people.

## Before the session

- Pick people who are comfortable with a computer but have never used Claude Code or built a web page.
- Ask them to bring their own laptop (so setup is real). Phones are welcome for task 1 and 2.
- Have the live link ready, and a place to save notes (one copy of the notes template below per person).
- Tell them: "I'm testing the website, not you. If something is confusing, that's the website's fault. Please think out loud."
- Ask permission before screen or audio recording. Record nothing without a clear yes.

## Your rules during the session

1. **Stay quiet.** Read the task aloud, then stop talking.
2. **Don't help** unless they have been stuck for **3 minutes or more**. When you do help, give the smallest hint, and write down exactly what you said.
3. **Don't explain the site** or defend it. "What did you expect to happen?" is fine.
4. **Write down everything:** every pause, every question they ask, every error message, every time they go back or give up, and anything they say out loud (use their words).
5. Time each task.

## The tasks (read each one aloud)

1. **Find an animation you'd use for something real in your life.** (About 10 minutes.) Start at the home page. They may use the Gallery, the Tour or the filters.
2. **Make it yours with the sliders.** (About 10 minutes.) Change at least three things and pick a color world. Then copy the prompt.
3. **Get it running on your computer with Claude Code.** (About 30 to 40 minutes.) They should use the Start here guide. Expect to see them install Claude Code, paste the prompt and open the result.
4. **Put it online and send me the link.** (About 15 to 20 minutes.) They should use the Put it online guide.

If a task takes more than twice its time, note where they are and move on to the next one.

## What to look for

- Where they look first, and what they click.
- Words they don't understand (this feeds the Words page).
- Places they scroll past something they needed.
- Errors and what they did next. Did they find the Fix it guide on their own?
- Whether the copied prompt worked on the first try.
- Any moment they smile, or say "oh, cool". Write those down too.

## Follow-up survey (5 questions, send right after)

1. In one sentence, what is peak for?
2. How easy was it to get from the site to a working page on your computer? (1 = very hard, 5 = very easy). What made it hard?
3. What was the most confusing moment?
4. Would you use peak again? What for?
5. What is the one thing you would change?

## After the sessions

- Put every place someone got stuck at the bottom of `docs/PLAN.md`, under "What testers taught us", as a new step with a "Done when" line.
- Fix problems that two or more people hit first.
- Don't change anything because of a single person's taste. Change it when a pattern shows up.
